Resources

Selected academic resources spanning research labs, scholarly writing, research communication, and research training. Maintained on a best-effort basis.

✍️ Academic Reading and Writing

  • How to Read a Paper by Prof. Srinivasan Keshav
    Describes a three-stage strategy for systematically reading research papers.

  • How to Read a Technical Paper by Prof. Jason Eisner
    Practical strategies for reading and analyzing technical research papers.

  • Write the Paper First by Prof. Jason Eisner
    Advice on structuring research by drafting the paper early.

  • A Writer’s Reference — Diana Hacker and Nancy Sommers
    A comprehensive handbook for developing professional and academic writing skills.

  • On Writing Well — William Zinsser
    A classic guide to nonfiction writing. Although nearly 50 years old, it remains an excellent resource for understanding writing principles (Part I) and methods (Part II). The author also discusses different forms of writing in Part III. As William Zinsser notes, “Learning is a tonic.”

  • Writing Tips by Prof. Jordan Boyd-Graber
    Practical guidance on academic writing and research communication.

🧭 Style Guides and Publication Standards

  • Awesome ML Conference Paper Writing Style Guide
    A practical resource outlining structure, clarity, and stylistic conventions for high-quality ML research papers.

  • IEEE Reference Guide
    Provides essential guidance on citations, abbreviations, formatting, and scholarly writing standards. IEEE Author Center source

  • IEEE Editorial Style Manual for Authors
    A detailed manual covering editorial conventions and best practices for technical writing. IEEE Author Center source

  • How to cite properly
    Different takes on doing citations right: Jordan Boyd-Graber on citation style · John Owens on common BibTeX errors · Jia-Bin Huang on citation etiquette · IEEE Citation Guidelines for formal reference

🎤 Research Communication

  • How to Speak — Prof. Patrick Winston (YouTube)
    A widely recommended lecture on structuring and delivering research presentations.

  • How to Ask a Question in Class by Prof. Jason Eisner
    Insightful advice on asking thoughtful, high-level academic questions.

  • Revising an Academic Poster — Prof. Jia-Bin Huang (Twitter Thread)
    Practical guidance on improving research posters for clarity and impact.

🎓 Research Skills and Mentorship

  • Being a Researcher by Prof. Carlo Ghezzi
    A reflective course on research mindset and academic professionalism.

    A curated directory of academic NLP research labs worldwide.
